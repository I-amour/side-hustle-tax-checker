📦 Shoebox — Help sheet

Two minutes of reading, then a lifetime of not typing your sales into a spreadsheet.

Filing your sales (the normal routine)

  1. Log in to vinted.co.uk in Chrome (the website, not the app — extensions can't run inside apps).
  2. Go to your own profile: click your avatar in the top corner, then My listings (sometimes shown as your wardrobe or "View my profile"). Your past items with the green Sold label live here.
  3. Scroll down through your listings first. This matters: Vinted only loads items as you scroll, so anything you haven't scrolled past isn't on the page yet and can't be filed.
  4. Press the green 📦 File sold items in Shoebox button (bottom-right of the page).
  5. Click the Shoebox icon in your Chrome toolbar to see your ledger, the £1,000 meter, and your totals.
Sold something for cash, or on another platform? Use the "add a sale by hand" row at the bottom of the popup. Shoebox doesn't judge where money comes from.

"No sold items here" — but I can see them!

  • Scroll, then press. Nine times out of ten this is it — the items weren't loaded into the page yet.
  • Make sure you're on your own listings page while logged in. Other people's profiles don't show sold items on the Vinted website.
  • Try My orders → Sold as an alternative page (profile menu → My orders, then the sold/selling side).
  • Refresh the page and try again — Vinted is a modern web app and occasionally the button loads before the page settles.
If the button says it found sold badges but no prices, that's Shoebox being honest that Vinted's page layout has changed shape on it. Email a screenshot of the page (blur anything private) to simi.enquiries@gmail.com and it'll get fixed — updates usually ship within a day or two.

Costs and profit

In the popup, each filed sale has a small cream box. Type what the item cost you (what you paid for it, or roughly what you spent making it) and your profit figure updates itself. Leave it blank for things that cost you nothing — old clothes you already owned, mostly.

The £1,000 meter, in one paragraph

UK sellers get a £1,000 trading allowance per tax year before HMRC needs to hear from them — but that only applies if you're trading (buying or making things to sell, or doing paid work). Just clearing out your own wardrobe? That's decluttering, and it isn't taxed at all, however much you sell. The meter watches the line for you either way, and the free checker gives you a proper verdict on your situation.

Filing your purchases (new!)

Open the popup and switch to the purchases tab. Go to your My orders → Purchased page on Vinted, scroll through it, then press "file this page as purchases" in the popup. Your bought items get their own ledger, their own total, and their own CSV — never mixed with your sales. You can also add purchases by hand.

Important: only use the purchase-filing button on your own Purchased page. On browsing pages it can't tell what you bought from what you merely looked at, and will cheerfully file your window shopping.

Exporting your records

Press export CSV in the popup. The file saves straight to your device and opens in Excel or Google Sheets. The columns are designed to drop directly into the Side Hustle Books spreadsheet, which does the full year-round bookkeeping if you want it — but the CSV is yours and works anywhere.

Your data

Everything lives on your own device. No account, no server, nothing transmitted, ever. Uninstalling deletes the lot, so export first if you're leaving. Full details in the privacy policy.

Keeping this free

Shoebox is free and stays free. If it saved your January and you fancy it, a coffee genuinely makes my week and funds the updates every time Vinted redecorates their website.
