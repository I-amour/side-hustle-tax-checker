Asking prices lie. Sold prices do not.

Anyone can list a coat at ninety pounds. That tells you nothing about whether a coat like that ever sells for ninety pounds. Comps reads eBay's completed sales, works out the typical price, subtracts the fees and postage everyone forgets, and tells you what you would genuinely keep.

It uses the median, not the average, on purpose. One person overpaying wildly for something should not convince you that yours is worth that much.

It also finds the ones going too cheap

Every search checks live listings as well. If somebody is selling the same thing for well under what it normally goes for, postage included, Comps puts it in front of you.

How it works

  1. Type what you are looking at. Brand, item, size. Fewer words beats a full description.
  2. Add what it costs you and roughly what postage will be.
  3. Press the button. Comps opens eBay's own sold search in the background, reads the real prices, closes the tab, and shows you the verdict.

It reads. It never bids, buys, lists or messages. Everything happens in your own browser, exactly as if you had run the search yourself, only faster and with the arithmetic done.
