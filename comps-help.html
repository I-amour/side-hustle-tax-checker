Comps: help sheet

Two minutes of reading, then better decisions in charity shops forever.

The basic routine

  1. Click the Comps icon in your Chrome toolbar.
  2. Type what the thing is. Brand, item, size is usually perfect.
  3. Add what it costs you and roughly what postage will run to.
  4. Press the button and wait a few seconds while it reads eBay's completed sales.

Searching well, which matters more than anything

The quality of your answer depends almost entirely on the words you type. eBay matches text, so a full description finds nothing while a tight one finds everything.

Do thisnike air force 1 uk 8
Not thiswhite trainers good condition barely worn size 8
Do thisnorth face nuptse 700 medium
Not thisblack puffer coat warm winter jacket

If you get very few results, drop a word. If the recent sales list shows the wrong kind of item, add one. Size matters for clothes and shoes. For electronics, model numbers beat everything.

Reading the numbers

  • The big figure is the median sold price: the middle of what things like this actually went for. It ignores one lunatic paying triple, which an average would not.
  • The bar shows the full range, with the shaded part covering the middle half of sales. A wide bar means condition and detail matter a lot for this item.
  • You would keep is the figure that decides things: sold price minus fees, postage and what you paid.

Leave itLess than the price of a coffee for all that effort.
Walk awayYou would lose money. Put it back.

No results came back

  • Use fewer words. Nine times out of ten this is it.
  • Check the item genuinely sells on eBay UK. Very local or very niche things sometimes do not.
  • Give it a moment and try again. Occasionally eBay is slow to render and the read times out.
  • If the numbers ever look obviously wrong, that usually means eBay has changed their page layout. Email me and it gets fixed.

Fees are an estimate. Comps assumes eBay's standard cut, deliberately rounded in eBay's favour so reality is usually kinder than the prediction. Your own account may differ, especially with a shop subscription or promoted listings. Check your seller fees for exact figures.

Your data

There is none to worry about. Comps stores nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. Full detail in the privacy policy.
